# Class 8. NLP for Social Good
Today's class explores how NLP can be applied for positive social impact. For example, we may want to prevent LLMs from producing harmful stereotypes ([Exercise 1](/book/class8/001_steering_vectors.ipynb)):

```{admonition} RESEARCH QUESTION
Can we compute steering vectors to guide LLMs away from producing harmful outputs, such as hateful or stereotypical content?
```

We will also explore how to make the NLP technology we develop both computationally efficient and accessible, including for languages beyond English ([Exercise 2](/book/class8/002_lora.ipynb)):

```{admonition} RESEARCH QUESTION
To what extent can parameter-efficient fine-tuning via LoRa produce an LLM capable of accurate English–Danish machine translation?
```


**As you will see, even approaches like steering vectors, applied with the best intentions, raise complex ethical questions.**
