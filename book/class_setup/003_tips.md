# Step 3. Re-run, don't start over at Step 1!
You might have done [Step 1](/book/class_setup/001_running_jobs.md) and [Step 2](/book/class_setup/002_jupyter.md) and closed your UCloud run afterwards. You don't need to configure everything from Step 1 again! You can re-run with the same settings :)

On the left sidebar, the last symbol in the top is for "jobs". Click on this (1) to see the Jobs page. Then, press on a previous job (2) and press **Run Again** (3):
